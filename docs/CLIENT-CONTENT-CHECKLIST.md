### For each project

- [ ] One wide establishing photo
- [ ] One clear equipment photo
- [ ] One diagnostic or work-in-progress photo
- [ ] One completed-work photo
- [ ] Optional before-and-after pair from the same angle

### Image standards

- Use the original file rather than a screenshot or social-media download.
- Prefer landscape images at 2000 pixels wide or larger.
- Do not apply filters, text or watermarks.
- Remove visible customer names, documents, access codes and license plates unless approved.
- Do not show unsafe work practices.
- Obtain customer or property permission before publishing identifiable locations.
- Name files descriptively, such as `cleveland-walk-in-cooler-compressor-before.jpg`.
