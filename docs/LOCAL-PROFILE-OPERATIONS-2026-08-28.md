## Post-service review request

Debynyhan owns the routine request process. Send one request only after the work is complete and the customer has had a reasonable chance to confirm the service outcome, normally the same day or next business day. Do not request only positive reviews, offer incentives or ask an employee to write a customer review.

Initial channel: email. SMS remains deferred until the business confirms that `216-703-3183` is monitored for texting and approves consent, staffing and retention rules.

Working email template:

> Subject: Thank you for choosing Eternity Mechanical Services
>
> Thank you for trusting Eternity Mechanical Services. If you have a moment, would you share an honest review of your experience? Your feedback helps local customers understand what to expect from our team.
>
> Leave a Google review: https://g.page/r/CYsWl6Bz9AJvEBM/review
>
> Thank you,  
> Eternity Mechanical Services  
> 216-703-3183

Log the request date and channel with the job record. Do not record or predict the customer's rating.

## Public review responses

Debynyhan drafts and publishes routine responses within two business days. Every response should:

1. Use the customer's displayed first name when appropriate.
2. Thank the customer and refer only to service details the customer made public or Eternity can safely confirm.
3. Avoid diagnoses, prices, addresses, access instructions, account details and private customer information.
4. Avoid invented outcomes, equipment claims or copy-and-paste language that does not match the review.
5. Invite future contact only when it is relevant.

Bernard reviews responses involving a technical dispute, safety issue, threat, legal claim, accusation of dishonesty, refund demand or significant service failure. For a critical review, acknowledge the concern without arguing, avoid confirming private job details and ask the reviewer to call `216-703-3183` so the matter can be reviewed offline. Do not promise compensation publicly.

Customer reviews may be added to the website only when the text, attribution and display permission are documented. Never rewrite a customer's claim to make it stronger.

## Weekly and monthly control cycle

A recurring check runs every Monday at 9:00 a.m. Eastern and reports findings in this task. It reviews:

- new or unreplied Google reviews;
- phone, website, regular and special hours;
- primary and secondary categories;
- the approved 20-city service area and confirmed services;
- unauthorized profile suggestions or edits;
- BuildZoom and Porch correction status.

The check does not publish photos, prices, offers, booking links, SMS changes, street addresses or unapproved claims, and it does not change an external profile without current-task approval.

On the first weekly check of each month, also record profile views, searches, calls, website clicks, new reviews and average rating in the progress tracker. Treat small numbers as a baseline, not a trend.
