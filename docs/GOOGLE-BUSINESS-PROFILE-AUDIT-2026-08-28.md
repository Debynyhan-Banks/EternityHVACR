- Charlotte Mancini: describes punctual inspection, explanation, honest repair options and pricing, correct work and improved system operation. The published owner reply says she received a “new HVAC system,” which is not supported by her review and should be corrected.

Recommended Charlotte reply:

> Thank you, Charlotte. We appreciate your detailed feedback and are glad our team explained the HVAC issue and repair options clearly, completed the work carefully and left the system operating better. Thank you for trusting Eternity Mechanical Services and for recommending us.

Use a tailored response for every review. Continue using the direct approved review link, never offer incentives, and make the review request part of the completed-job workflow.

Official references: [Tips to get more reviews](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122?hl=en) and [Manage customer reviews](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474050?hl=en).
